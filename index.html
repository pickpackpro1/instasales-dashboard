How this tool works & known limitations

  • Everything runs locally in your browser - no data is uploaded anywhere.
  • Amazon: the Business Report is aggregated per ASIN - no per-day data, no refunds, no FBA fee detail. Referral %% comes from the master sheet (fallback slider in Settings); use the Est. Refund slider to approximate returns. A Settlement/Date-Range report can make Amazon exact later.
  • eBay: "eBay Fees" = Total selling costs from the Listings Sales Report minus ad fees (so ads aren't double-counted). Refunds come from the Transaction report. Account-level fees (shop subscription) should be added on the OpEx page.
  • TikTok: same verified engine as the TikTok-only dashboard - revenue = Order Amount − Refund prorated per SKU; per-SKU referral %% from the master sheet.
  • Date filter (Settings) applies to TikTok only - Amazon/eBay reports are pre-aggregated. Export all channels for the same month for clean comparisons.
  • ACOS = ad spend ÷ ad-attributed sales. TACOS = ad spend ÷ total revenue.
  • VAT: revenue is treated as VAT-inclusive (UK). Net profit is shown ex-VAT; tick which costs carry reclaimable input VAT in Settings.
